Pillar 01 · Due Diligence

Land Encumbrance Certificate (EC) Auditing

2–4 Working Days

An Encumbrance Certificate verifies whether a property is free from liabilities, bank mortgages, court attachments, or conflicting prior conveyances. We conduct deep-record verification across Tirunelveli sub-registrar jurisdictions covering up to 30+ retrospective years.

What We Check & Deliver
  • Verification up to 30 to 40 years retrospective index
  • Digital and physical extract from Sub-Registrar Office
  • Cross-matching survey numbers with parent title deeds
  • Comprehensive title search opinion prior to purchase
Documents Required
  • Copy of prior title deed or registered document number
  • Village name, Taluk, and Survey / Sub-division number
  • Exact four-sided boundary schedule details
  • Applicant identity proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID)
Apply for EC Verification
Pillar 02 · Conveyance

Sale Deed Drafting & Sub-Registrar Filing

5–8 Working Days

A legally sound Sale Deed protects your lifelong property rights. We draft custom conveyance clauses, verify seller lineage, compute guideline values, handle e-stamping, and personally accompany clients at the Sub-Registrar desk for registration and biometrics.

What We Check & Deliver
  • Verification of seller’s clear title and ownership continuity
  • Precise boundary, guideline value, and indemnity clauses
  • Preparation of e-stamp papers and official treasury challans
  • Advocate representation during sub-registrar biometrics
Documents Required
  • Original parent deeds & consecutive link documents
  • Current Nil Encumbrance Certificate
  • Patta passbook & latest property tax receipts
  • PAN, Aadhaar and photos of buyer, seller, and 2 witnesses
Draft Your Sale Deed
Pillar 03 · Revenue

Nagal, Adangal & Patta Transfer Processing

7–14 Working Days

Agricultural landholdings and residential plots require authenticated revenue records. We interface directly with the Taluk Office, VAO (Village Administrative Officer), and Zonal Tahsildars to process Patta transfers, certified Nagal copies, Adangal extracts, and FMB sketches.

What We Check & Deliver
  • Individual & Joint Patta name transfer mutation filings
  • Certified Nagal (true certified copy of registered deeds)
  • Adangal crop & ownership verification extracts
  • FMB (Field Measurement Book) boundary sketch procurement
Documents Required
  • Copy of registered sale deed or settlement deed
  • Existing Patta number and revenue survey detail
  • Recent land tax receipt (Kist)
  • Legal heir certificate (in case of ancestral property)
Request Revenue Records

FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

What is an Encumbrance Certificate (EC) and why do I need it?

An EC is an official record of all transactions registered against a property over a specific period. It confirms the property is free from legal liabilities and mortgages, and is needed for purchases, home loans and title transfers.

How long does a sale deed registration take?

Typically 7–15 working days, depending on document readiness and sub-registrar appointment availability. We keep this as short as possible while ensuring legal accuracy.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. We offer a completely free initial consultation for all new clients, so you can understand your requirements before making any decision or payment.

Do you handle Patta transfer and Chitta records?

Yes. We handle Patta transfer, Chitta, Adangal and FMB (Field Measurement Book) extracts for lands within Tirunelveli district and surrounding areas.

Can I track my document status?

Yes. Your assigned file officer provides regular updates, and you can contact us anytime by WhatsApp or phone for the current status of your case.

What areas do you serve?

We mainly serve Tirunelveli, Palayamkottai, Tenkasi and surrounding districts. Major cases in other Tamil Nadu districts can be handled on request.
